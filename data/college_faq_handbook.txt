3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ADMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- B.Tech Programs:
  Passed 10+2 (or equivalent) examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry/Computer Science/Biology. Minimum aggregate of 60% marks (55% for reserved category candidates) in 10+2. A valid JEE Main or State CET rank is required.

- BCA Program:
  Passed 10+2 examination with Mathematics or Computer Science/Information Practices as a subject, securing at least 50% marks in aggregate.

- M.Tech Program:
  B.E. / B.Tech degree in relevant engineering branch with a minimum of 60% aggregate or CGPA of 6.5 on a 10-point scale. GATE-qualified applicants receive first preference and monthly stipend.

- MBA Program:
  Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Valid score in CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT.

- MCA Program:
  BCA or B.Sc in Computer Science / IT / Mathematics with at least 50% marks in aggregate.

4. ADMISSION PROCESS & STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 - Online Application:
Visit the official college portal (www.college-admissions.edu) and fill out the online admission inquiry form. Submit application fee of Rs. 1,000 online.

Step 2 - Merit List & Counselling:
Merit lists are published based on entrance examination scores and qualifying marks. Shortlisted candidates are invited for offline or virtual counselling rounds.

Step 3 - Document Verification:
Candidates must report to the Admissions Cell with original and self-attested copies of all mandatory certificates.

Step 4 - Seat Allotment & Provisional Admission:
Upon successful verification, the candidate chooses their branch based on availability and rank. A provisional seat allotment letter is issued.

Step 5 - Fee Payment:
Deposit the first-semester tuition fee within 5 working days of seat allotment to confirm the admission.

5. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ADMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Applicants must submit 2 sets of self-attested photocopies along with original documents for verification:
1. Class 10 (SSC) Marksheet and Passing Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth).
2. Class 12 (HSC) Marksheet and Passing Certificate.
3. Graduation Marksheets (all semesters) and Degree/Provisional Certificate (for PG courses).
4. Valid Entrance Exam Scorecard (JEE Main / GATE / CAT / MAT / CET).
5. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Character Certificate from the last attended institute.
6. Migration Certificate (for candidates from outside state/board).
7. Caste / Category Certificate and Income Certificate (for SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates).
8. Domicile Certificate (if claiming state quota).
9. Copy of Aadhaar Card of student and parent.
10. Five recent passport-size color photographs.
11. Anti-ragging affidavit signed by student and parent (on Rs. 10 stamp paper or online undertaking).

7. EXAMINATION SCHEDULE, RULES & EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Semester Structure: The academic year consists of two semesters (Odd: August to December; Even: January to May).
- Examination Dates:
  - Odd Semester End Exams: Typically commence in the first week of December.
  - Even Semester End Exams: Typically commence in the second week of May.
  - Mid-Term / Sessional Exams: Held in October (Odd semester) and March (Even semester).
- Attendance Requirement: A minimum of 75% attendance in theory lectures and 80% in laboratory practicals is strictly mandatory to appear in end-semester examinations.
- Evaluation Weightage:
  - Internal Assessment: 40% (Mid-term tests, lab practical evaluations, assignments, quizzes, and attendance).
  - End-Semester University Exam: 60%.
- Passing Standard: Minimum 40% in each subject (internal + external combined) and overall semester grade point average (SGPA) of 5.0.
